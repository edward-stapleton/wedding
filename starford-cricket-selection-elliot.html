Timings

We’d love you to arrive at the pitch any time from 14.30–15.00 on Friday afternoon, with the game set to start at 15.30 after some catching drills, shoulder-loosening in the nets, the captains’ toss and, of course, the anthems. Please let us know if you’ll struggle to make it for then. The game should be finished by 19.30 latest, with a short ceremony to follow before leaving you to your own plans for Friday evening.

Travel & Access

There is unfortunately no parking available at the ground for guests, and street parking in central Oxford is pretty expensive as the council are quite militant about car use. Therefore, if you can, we’d recommend walking or catching a bus/cab (including the Park and Ride if coming from out-of-town).

Format

The game will take the format of a standard 11-a-side, 20-over match, with 20 six-ball overs per innings. To save time, we’ll bowl all 20 overs from one end of the wicket. Batsmen will retire at 30 (with the possibility of returning to the crease if the rest of their team is skittled), and each bowler will bowl a maximum of four overs.

Kit & Changing Facilities

Our intention is that all players will wear cricket whites (i.e. shirt and trousers). If you don’t have access to cricket whites, please could you reply with your sizing (i.e. S/M/L/XL for shirt and trousers) so that we can arrange some for you? Sports trainers are absolutely fine to play in, and bats, pads, gloves and helmets will be available to borrow if you aren’t able to bring any. Crucially, we’ll have boxes for you to protect your crown jewels with, but please remember to bring a suitable jock strap, sock or just an additional pair of tight boxers to avoid any unnecessary intimacy. Please note that there are some nice changing and shower cubicles within the pavilion for your use if desired, and plenty of space to securely store any bags if you’re coming directly to the cricket before checking into your accommodation.

Pitch

After the drought in UK this summer, the outfield at Balliol has started to look and feel more Caribbean than County Championship. While there is some drizzle in the forecast for the middle of next week, we wanted to advise caution on the outfield, despite the obvious pride that you’ll feel to be representing your country. As impressive as a one-handed diving catch at cover point or a sliding stop on the boundary can be, we're eager to avoid any injuries that may inhibit your enjoyment of the Starford Invitational after-party (also known as our wedding day) on the Saturday.

Refreshments

A match tea will be laid on for players and spectators alike to reward the hard graft in the outfield. This will be accompanied by liquid refreshments in the form of draught beer, ice-cold cans of Shandy Shack, jugs of Pimm’s and soft drinks.
